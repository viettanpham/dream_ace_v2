### Consumables (Separate from `ITEM_TEMPLATES`)
Consumables are NOT stored as a template catalog array; 4 kinds are defined via `ConsumableKind` (`src/types/game.ts:409-413`) and generated/priced solely in War Points inside `SHOP_STOCK_CONFIG.wpOffers` (`src/data/shopConfig.ts:74-102`) & `generateWpMarketOffers` (`src/engine/shop.ts:154-234`):
- `equipment_crate`: `35 / 55 / 95 / 160 WP` (by `minRarity`: common/rare/epic/legendary; common weight = 0 in WP market)
- `enchant_stone`: `45 WP` (`+20%` next enchant success bonus)
- `respec_token`: `40 WP` (resets active Gear allocated stat points)
- `exp_boost`: `50 WP` (`+50%` EXP for `5` battles)

## 3. Candidate Formula Fit & >25% Delta Check

Candidate formula fitted to current table medians:
```
price = round(baseBySlot[slot] * tierFactor[tier] * rarityFactor[rarity] * (1 + (rollQuality - 50) * 0.0028))
```
- `baseBySlot`: `main_weapon: 184`, `sub_weapon: 173`, `armor: 194`, `engine: 189`, `cpu: 179`, `accessory: 173` (exact current T1 Common Q50 medians; equivalently `180/170/190/185/175/170 * 1.02`)
- `tierFactor`: `{ 1: 1.00, 2: 1.74, 3: 2.54, 4: 3.80 }` (median tier multipliers across all 96 cells)
- `rarityFactor`: `{ common: 1.00, rare: 1.65, epic: 2.75, legendary: 4.60 }` (matches `BALANCE_CONFIG.rarityPriceMultiplier`)
- Quality bonus at `rollQuality = 50`: `1 + (50 - 50) * 0.0028 = 1.00` (ranges `0.8628` at Q1 to `1.14` at Q100, matching current `0.88..1.16` spread)

Cells differing by `> 25%` from candidate:
- **At cell-median level**: **0 / 96** cells differ by `> 25%` (worst cell medians are `engine T4` at `-22.2%` and `armor T4` at `-20.6%`).
- **At individual template-instance level (12 / 96 cells)**: `armor T2` (`min +30.0..+30.5%`), `armor T4` (`max -33.1%`), and `engine T4` (`max -31.4%`) across all 4 rarities exceed 25% due to missing T2/T4 templates falling back to T1/T3 templates rescaled by `BALANCE_CONFIG.tierPriceScale`.
