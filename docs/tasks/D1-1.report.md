## 4. Top Outliers (Worst First)
1. **Cross-template price inversion in `armor`/`engine` (T1-tpl > T3-tpl at EVERY tier!)**: Missing T2/T4 templates cause `generateItemInstance({ slot, tier })` (`src/engine/equipment.ts:82-86,194-196`) to rescale T1 & T3 templates by `tierPriceScale[t-1]/tierPriceScale[tpl.tier-1]`, while `ITEM_TEMPLATES` (`src/data/items.ts`) grows slower (`T3/T1 = ~2.4×`) than `tierPriceScale[2]` (`3.2×`). Result: at T4, T1-sourced `armor` = `1047..1102 CR` (`5067 CR` Leg) vs T3-sourced `armor` = `757..809 CR` (`3721 CR` Leg) — a **1.46× gap**; at T2, T3-sourced `armor` (`259 CR`) is **28% cheaper** than T1-sourced `armor` (`359 CR`).
2. **200-roll tier inversion (`armor` T2 max > `armor` T3 min)**: `armor` Common T2 hits `421 CR` (`421 > 400` T3 min), Rare T2 hits `681 CR` (`681 > 668` T3 min), caused by T1→T2 `1.85×` upscaling + random `rollQuality` (`equipment.ts:82-97,194-198`).
3. **`armor` & `engine` T4 inflated ~38% above all other T4 slots**: Median Common T4 is `928` (`armor`) / `923` (`engine`) vs `622..694` (`sub_weapon..main_weapon`) because `tierPriceScale[3]=5.4` applies to `armor`/`engine` crates (`useGameStore.ts:2072-2078`) while native T4 templates only scale `~3.60–3.83×` over T1 (`items.ts:253,345,724,813`).
4. **Shop vs `generateItemInstance` inaccessible tiers + mismatched floors**: `generateCategorizedShopStock` (`shop.ts:117-130`) locks out T2/T4 `armor`/`engine` (`tierPriceMult` never runs in shop) and enforces `Math.max(40, ...)` (`shop.ts:141`) while `equipment.ts:203` enforces `Math.max(60, ...)`.
5. **Affixes & special traits contribute 0 CR to price**: A Legendary with 3 high affixes and traits (`longRange`, `splashPct`) costs identically to a traitless item of the same `basePrice` & `rollQuality` (`equipment.ts:194-210`).
